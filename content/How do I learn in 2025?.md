With the rising of AI and so much resources available in the Internet, my approach to learning has changed so much. From shitty => simple => boring => complex => second brain (complex rules) => simple zettelkasten

I am no productivity guru and will share only from my perspective. The word "Learning" has multiple interpretations,

> **Psychological** => The process of acquiring new knowledge, behaviors, skills, values, or preferences.
> **Biological** => The strengthening of neural pathways as a result of repetitive experiences.
> **Philosophical** => The pursuit of understanding and wisdom by integrating information into a coherent framework.
